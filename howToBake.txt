How to Bake a Texture:

1 - Make model
2 - Select Object, click Materials tab (checkered red sphere)
3 - New Material (may take a while to load)
4 - Change base color to desired 
5 - Go to UV Editing tab at top of screen
6 - "A" key to select all
7 - "U", then select "Smart UV Project"
8 - Change Island Margins to 0.1
9 - Unwrap
10 - Click Shading tab at top of screen. Zoom in/out if needed to see object
11 - Can change roughness, etc.
	- Make sure metallic is 0, otherwise will come out black
	- Can right click and add different texture modifiers under Texture tab
	- Can drag different nodes to inputs of the Principled BSDF node to modify
12 - When done, click the Render tab on right area. Shaped like grey camera back
13 - Change Render Engine to Cycles
14 - Open Bake tab below
15 - Change Bake Type to Diffuse
16 - Disable Direct and Indirect lighting
17 - Right click shader node window, under textures tab, add Image Texture with a new, blank, image
18 - Ensure object is selected in Layout Screen, then return to Shading
19 - Ensure the new Image Texture box is selected with a white border 
20 - Click "Bake" under bake tab
21 - Drag new image output color to input base color in Principled BSDF, detatch al other nodes from input
22 - Ensure new image is saved (must be saved separately from blend file)

23 - Export as FBX for Unity
24 - When import to Unity, Drag in FBX and image texture
	- Image texture SHOULD auto-apply
